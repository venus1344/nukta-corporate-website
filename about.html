Our Purpose & Belief System

We believe that data, when transformed into meaningful stories, has the power to improve lives, influence decisions, and drive positive change.

We exist to:

  • Empower individuals, organisations, and communities through data and digital storytelling.
  • Promote transparency, accountability, and evidence-based decision-making.
  • Democratise access to data, technology, and storytelling tools, so that knowledge is not only for a few, but for everyone.
  • Strengthen journalism, communication, and public awareness through accurate, original, and ethical storytelling.
  • Use stories for social good — advancing education, business growth, environmental awareness, innovation, and sustainable development.

We believe that informed people make better choices, and better choices build stronger societies.

We are data-first storytellers.

Every story we tell is guided by facts, verified data, and deep analysis — not speculation.

We combine data, journalism, design, and technology under one roof.

From data collection to visualization to publishing, we offer a complete storytelling ecosystem.

We make data accessible to everyone.

Through Swahili and simple digital formats, we turn complex information into clear, relatable stories.

We provide affordable, high-quality data visualization and infographics.

This allows even small organisations to communicate big ideas with impact.

We build capacity, not just content.

Through training, mentorship, and hands-on support, we equip others with practical data and storytelling skills.

We serve both media and organisations

We work with journalists, NGOs, businesses, researchers, and institutions to amplify impact through data.
