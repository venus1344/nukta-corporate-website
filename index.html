Nukta Africa is a digital company focused on: - Fact-checking in journalism - Training journalists - Digital storytelling and data-driven narratives - Improving lives through data and digital storytelling

Our Products & Services

We offer digital and data storytelling trainings

We believe in evidence-based journalism and digital storytelling. We offer training and continuous mentorship to individual journalists, newsrooms, NGOs, and companies on data journalism, multimedia storytelling, and emerging digital techniques.

Our training packages includes:
  • Data journalism & Fact-checking
  • Multimedia storytelling
  • Narrative storytelling for advocacy
  • Digital Content & Newsletter Management
  • Data Visualisation & Infographics

We provide analytical news content

Through our independent online news portals, Nukta Habari (nukta.co.tz) and Jiko Point (jikopoint.co.tz), we deliver fresh, analytical and data-driven stories on business, technology, education, and clean cooking.

Our news platforms cover:
  • Business & Technology reporting
  • Education & Safari coverage
  • Clean cooking & Food safety
  • Data-driven analysis & insights
  • Evidence-based journalism
