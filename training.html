Our Training Courses



Data Journalism

3–14 days

In today’s world, data is crucial in our daily lives. Journalists too are required to cope with a data-driven world that is why we are offering this data journalism course. Upon finishing the basics of this course, you will be able to find, extract, convert non-machine readable data into machine readable formats, analyse the data to obtain hidden stories, visualise your story and create engaging data-driven narratives. This course is classified into three levels which are basic, intermediate and advanced level.

Fact Checking

Full Time-9 Weeks

We are in a world where anyone has the power to publish information about anything and at any time which has a devastating impact on the public. Journalists and communication personnel are key on debunking misinformation. This course provides key skills to trainees on best techniques and open source verification tools that helps to verify texts, images, videos, websites and social media accounts. Apart from learning tools and techniques, you will be able to write and present a story which debunks misinformation.

Narrative Storytelling for Advocacy

Full Time-9 Weeks

In this course, we provide storytelling techniques which can help people working in civil society organisations (CSOs) to develop more impactful advocacy campaigns on social media, mainstream and blogs. We dive deeper into data and digital storytelling techniques which deliver the campaign message in a truly engaging manner. Nowadays, advocacy is more than telling people to change, it is about influencing people to join you in changing the world

Digital Storytelling

Full Time-9 Weeks

This course we introduce participants to Multimedia storytelling tools and techniques. The course includes a quick overview of a number of online tools both open source and paid, that will assist journalists and communicators to tell engaging stories in simple ways. The course also includes hands-on practical sessions to allow participants to become familiar with these tools and techniques to produce impactful and engaging content.
